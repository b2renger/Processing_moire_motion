Moiré motion

Turn a short video into a barrier-grid animation. Print the combined image, print the striped grid on a transparent sheet, lay it on top and slide it: the picture moves.

Drop a video or frames

1Frames

You can also drop files on the preview. Several images become the frames, in file-name order.

Scrub the video, then set the start and end of one loop. Fewer frames give a brighter, clearer animation.

2Pattern

Each frame shows through one clear strip; the rest of the grid is black. Thin strips hide the trick better but need sharper printing.

3Print

Print both at 100% scale. The grid is larger than the image so it keeps covering it while it moves; line up their centres.

Curved and radial grids follow FabObscura (Sethapakdi et al., UIST 2025). Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Source on GitHub.
